Masjid Ki Importance Aur Ba-Jamat Namaz Ki Fazilat

Islam me masjid ko Allah ka ghar aur musalmanon ka Ijtimaayi markaz kaha gaya hai. Ba-jamat namaz ada karne ki fazilat zati namaz se 27 darjay zyada hoti hai. Jab musalman kisi naye shehar me ya safar me hote hain toh qareebi masjid ka pata lagana zaroori hota hai.
