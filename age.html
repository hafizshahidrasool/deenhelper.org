Hijri Umar Aur Gregorian Umar Me Farq

Islamic Hijri calendar me ek saal me 354 ya 355 din hotay hain, jabke Gregorian calendar me 365 din hotay hain. Is 11 din ke saalana farq ki waja se har 33 Gregorian saalon baad Hijri umar taqreeban 1 saal zyada hoti hai.

Deeni zimmadariyon aur takleef shar'i me Hijri umar ki bari importance hoti hai.
