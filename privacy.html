Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy: Your Data Security

Deen Helper par aap ki privacy hamari pehli priority hai.

1. Information We Collect

Hum is website par kisi user se account ya signup karne ki shart nahi rakhte. Hamare 10 tools istemal karne ke liye kisi personal info ya bank password ki zaroorat nahi hoti.

2. Local Browser Storage Usage

Aap ka Tasbeeh counter, target selection, ya GPS location permissions aap ke apne mobile browser ki LocalStorage me mehfooz rehte hain. Yeh data hamare server par store nahi hota aur aap browser history clear kar ke ise delete kar sakte hain.

3. Google AdSense & Cookies Policy

Hamari website monetization ke liye Google AdSense ads istemal karti hai. Google DoubleClick DART cookies ke zariye users ki browser history ke mutabiq relevant ads dikhata hai. Users Google Ad Settings me ja kar personalized ads ko disable kar sakte hain.

4. Google Forms Data Privacy

About aur Contact pages par embedded Google Forms ka data directly Google's secure servers par bheja jata hai. Hum is data ko kisi marketing company ko nahi bechte.
