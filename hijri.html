What Is Islamic Date (Islamic Calendar Kya Hai)

Islamic Calendar jise Hijri Calendar bhi kaha jata hai, Nabi Akram Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ki Makkah se Madinah Hijrat ke saal se shuru hua. Yeh calendar chand ki harkat par munhasir hota hai. Musalmanon ke tamaam deeni teyohar jaise Ramadan, Eid ul Fitr, Eid ul Adha, aur Hajj ki tareekhein isi Hijri calendar ke mutabiq tay hoti hain.

Modern duniya me Aangrezi Gregorian calendar istemal hota hai, is liye dono calendars ke darmayan tareekh convert karne ki zaroorat hoti hai.
