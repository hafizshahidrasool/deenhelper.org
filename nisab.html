Nisab Ki Sharae Miqdar

Shariah me Zakat farz hone ke liye Nisab ki shart rakhi gayi hai. Sone ka nisab 7.5 tola (87.48 grams) hai aur Chandi ka nisab 52.5 tola (612.36 grams) hai. Agar kisi ke paas cash ya trading goods hon toh Chandi ke nisab ki monetary value dekhi jati hai.
